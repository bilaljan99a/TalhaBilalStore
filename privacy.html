TALHA BILAL STORE

Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect when you place an order or contact Talha Bilal Store, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we may collect your name, phone number, complete delivery address, city and other delivery details you provide. Optional information such as email address or nearest landmark may also be provided during checkout.

How We Use Your Information

We use customer information to process and confirm orders, arrange delivery, contact customers about their orders, provide customer support and operate the store.

Cash on Delivery

Talha Bilal Store uses Cash on Delivery. We do not require card or online payment details to place a normal Cash on Delivery order through the website.

Information Sharing

We may share the delivery information necessary to fulfill an order with the courier or delivery service handling that parcel. We do not intentionally sell customer information to third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the customer information used for order processing and support. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so customers should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through contact channels.

Customer Requests

If you have a question about information associated with your order, contact us and include your order number where possible so we can identify the relevant order.
