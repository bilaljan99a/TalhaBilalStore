Return Policy

Please review the following return conditions for your order.

Return Within 7 Days

If you need to return a product, the request must be made within 7 days of delivery.

Original Packaging Required

The product must be returned in its original packaging and should be in the condition in which it was received.

Food Product Returns

For hygiene and food-safety reasons, opened or used food products may not be eligible for return, except where the product is damaged, incorrect or has a verified defect.

Return Request

Please contact us with your order number and details of the issue. We may request clear photos of the product and packaging before approving a return.
