HOW TO ORDER

Ordering is simple.

No account, card or complicated checkout. Pick your product, enter your delivery details and send the order to us on WhatsApp.

01

Choose your product

Open All Products and select the Mango Pulp pack size you want. You can use Buy Now — COD or add products to your cart.

02

Set your quantity

Use the minus and plus buttons in the shopping cart to choose exactly how many packs you need.

03

Enter delivery details

Provide your full name, phone number, complete address and city. These are the only details needed for delivery.

04

Confirm on WhatsApp

Tap Place Order on WhatsApp. Your order summary and delivery information will be prepared for us to receive and confirm.

Cash on DeliveryDelivery fee is Rs. 200 and is shown separately at checkout. You pay when the order is delivered.
