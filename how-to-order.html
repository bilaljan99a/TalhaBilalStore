HOW TO ORDER

Ordering is simple.

Choose your products, add them to your cart and complete the COD checkout directly on our website. No account or online payment is required.

01

Choose your product

Open All Products and select the Mango Pulp pack size you want. You can buy a product directly or add it to your cart.

02

Set your quantity

Use the minus and plus controls to select the quantity you need. You can add different pack sizes to the same cart.

03

Review your cart

Open your cart to check the products, quantities, product subtotal and applicable delivery fee. The final payable total is shown before checkout.

04

Enter delivery details

Enter your full name, phone number, complete address and city. Then submit the order using Place Order.

05

Order confirmed

After successful submission, you will see an order confirmation/thank-you screen. Your order is saved in our system for processing and delivery.

Cash on DeliveryNo advance online payment is required. The applicable delivery fee is shown separately at checkout and the complete payable amount is displayed before you place the order.
