How to Make Mango Drink from Mango Pulp Premix – Easy Homemade Recipe

Make a refreshing mango drink at home with a simple blend of mango pulp premix, water and sugar. This guide gives you the practical ratio, blending method and serving tips for a smooth, chilled drink.

If you have mango pulp premix at home and want a consistent mango drink without preparing fresh mangoes every time, the process is straightforward. The key is to blend the premix with water and sugar together, then chill the finished drink before serving.

Simple 1 KG Recipe

1 KGMango pulp premix
7 LClean drinking water
600–800 gSugar, according to taste

Best starting point: use around 600g sugar, blend and taste. Increase gradually up to about 800g if you prefer a sweeter drink.

How to Make Mango Drink Step by Step

  1. Measure the water: Use about 7 litres of clean drinking water.
  2. Add the mango pulp premix: Put the premix into a suitable blender or large food-safe mixing container.
  3. Add water and sugar: Add the water and your chosen amount of sugar. Start with around 600g sugar and adjust according to taste.
  4. Blend thoroughly: Blend the mango premix, water and sugar together until the mixture is evenly combined and smooth.
  5. Taste and adjust: If you want more sweetness, add sugar gradually, up to around 800g for the 7-litre preparation, then blend again.
  6. Chill and serve: Refrigerate the prepared drink until properly chilled. Add ice to individual glasses when serving if desired.

Why Blend the Premix with Water and Sugar?

Blending helps distribute the mango base and sugar evenly through the water. Adding the sugar during the blending stage also makes it easier to create a consistent taste throughout the batch.

How Much Sugar Should You Add?

The right amount depends on personal taste. For approximately 7 litres of water with 1 kg of mango pulp premix, a practical range is 600g to 800g of sugar. Start at the lower end, taste the drink, and increase gradually if you prefer more sweetness.

There is no need to add the full 800g immediately. Starting with 600g gives you room to adjust the sweetness without making the whole batch overly sweet.

How to Serve Mango Drink

For the best refreshing result, chill the prepared drink before serving. Pour into clean glasses and add ice if desired. The drink is suitable for everyday family servings as well as larger gatherings.

Fresh Mango vs Mango Pulp Premix

Fresh mango is whole fruit that you peel, remove the stone from and blend yourself. Mango pulp premix is a prepared mango-based drink product designed to be mixed with water and sugar. They are different products and should not be treated as identical.

Tips for a Better Mango Drink

  • Use clean drinking water for the preparation.
  • Start with about 600g sugar and adjust gradually instead of adding 800g immediately.
  • Blend the premix, water and sugar together thoroughly.
  • Chill the prepared drink before serving for a more refreshing taste.
  • Add ice to individual glasses so the whole batch does not become diluted as ice melts.
  • Keep leftover prepared drink covered and refrigerated and follow the storage guidance on the product packaging.

Can You Prepare Mango Drink for a Party?

Yes. A premix can be convenient when you need several servings because you can prepare a larger batch instead of making individual drinks. It can work well for family gatherings, birthday parties, summer get-togethers and other casual events.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much water is needed for 1 kg mango pulp premix?

Use approximately 7 litres of water for 1 kg of mango pulp premix in this preparation.

How much sugar should I add?

Start with around 600g and adjust according to taste, up to about 800g for the 7-litre preparation.

Should I blend the mango premix with water?

Yes. Blend the mango premix with the water and sugar together so the batch is evenly mixed.

Can I make a smaller batch?

Yes. Reduce the ingredients proportionally while keeping the same basic ratio.

Should I add ice to the whole batch?

It is better to chill the prepared drink first and add ice to individual glasses when serving, so the batch does not become diluted.
